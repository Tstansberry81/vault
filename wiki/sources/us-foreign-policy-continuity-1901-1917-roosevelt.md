# US Foreign Policy Continuity 1901–1917: Roosevelt, Taft, and Wilson

This note argues that American foreign policy changed only superficially between 1901 and 1917, because the underlying goal — spreading American democratic ideals — remained constant across administrations. The shift was methodological: Roosevelt pursued that goal through imperial protection (the Roosevelt Corollary), while Wilson pursued it through voluntary democratic formation (Moral Diplomacy). Taft's role and lasting impact on this arc is left as an open question.

## Key points
- American foreign policy from 1901–1917 was fundamentally continuous, not discontinuous
- The [[Roosevelt Corollary]] extended the [[Monroe Doctrine]] by asserting the US had a right/duty to intervene to protect Latin American democracies
- [[Moral Diplomacy]] (Wilson) still aimed to spread democracy but shifted toward voluntary self-governance rather than imposed protection
- Both approaches are described as "intertwined" — imperialism and spreading democracy were not opposites but co-dependent strategies
- The core variable that changed was *method*, not *goal*
- Open question raised: did [[Taft]]'s presidency (Dollar Diplomacy) meaningfully alter the long-run trajectory of US foreign policy?

## Entities & concepts
- [[Theodore Roosevelt]] — 26th president; authored the Roosevelt Corollary justifying US intervention in Latin America to uphold democracy
- [[Roosevelt Corollary]] — 1904 extension of the Monroe Doctrine asserting US policing authority in the Western Hemisphere
- [[Monroe Doctrine]] — foundational US policy opposing European interference in the Americas; basis for later corollaries
- [[Woodrow Wilson]] — 28th president; introduced Moral Diplomacy as a softer but still democracy-spreading foreign policy
- [[Moral Diplomacy]] — Wilson's approach favoring voluntary democratic self-governance over direct imperial intervention
- [[William Howard Taft]] — 27th president; associated with Dollar Diplomacy; his long-run policy impact is the note's central open question
- [[Stephen Kinzer]] — author of *Overthrow*, cited on p. 64 re: the Roosevelt Corollary's imperialist logic
