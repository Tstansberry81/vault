# 75 Hard 2025

An Apple Note containing Traveler's personal self-improvement regimen for 2025, written in his characteristic direct, unsparing tone.

## Full content (verbatim)

> no nic
> no drinks
> no porn/jer
> no weed
> workout 5 days a week (don't matter how long, just do it)
> - legs, ARMS, BACK focus
> <2000 cals a day (ideally <1500)
> - 150g protein daily
> **log EVERYTHING (even and ESPECIALLY if it's bad or over budget; no shame, just consistency)**
> lock in on school (ib diploma on the line. physics needs to be priority, along with maintains cum laude society)
> read EVERYNIGHT (don't matter how long, just do it)
> if I (ME) cannot do this, I am generationally cooked forever
> consistency is key. if I can't do something that day, make it up w something else productive
> < 3 hours daily of PHONE screen time (gd takes priority, along with doing school work)
> no insta*

## Key specifics
- **Substances banned:** nicotine, alcohol, weed, porn/masturbation
- **Fitness:** 5 days/week; emphasis on legs, arms, back
- **Nutrition:** under 2,000 kcal/day (target: under 1,500); 150g protein/day
- **Academic:** IB Diploma explicitly at stake; [[IB Physics (HL)]] named as top priority; maintaining cum laude society membership
- **Screen time:** under 3 hours/day of phone; [[Gaming and PC Setup|GD (gaming)]] and schoolwork are permitted carve-outs
- **Reading:** every night, no minimum length
- **Instagram:** banned (asterisk suggests partial exception or caveat — unclear)
- **Logging ethos:** radical honesty — log failures without shame

## Tone note
The phrase *"if I (ME) cannot do this, I am generationally cooked forever"* is characteristic of his self-motivating catastrophizing: he uses high-stakes language not as genuine despair but as a rhetorical device to override inertia. See also [[Self-Discipline and Goals]].
