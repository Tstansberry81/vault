# 75 Medium

A short Apple Note listing the rules for Traveler's self-designed "75 Medium" challenge — a lighter variant of the popular "75 Hard" regimen.

## The rules (verbatim)

- Work out 5 days a week
- No porn
- No smoking
- No vaping
- No drinking
- Read 30 minutes daily
- Lock in for SAT
- < 3 hours of screen time
- Eat well
- \> 1500 calories a day
- 100 g protein

## Notes on content

- **No weed** is absent here, unlike in prior regimens ([[75 hard 2025]]). This may be a lighter or later-stage version, or simply an omission.
- The **SAT** call-out is notable: this is likely timed to a test-prep window, placing the note plausibly in 2024–25 (junior/senior year prep cycle). Cross-reference [[College Search]].
- The calorie floor (>1500) and protein target (100 g) suggest a *body-composition* goal — likely lean muscle, not just general health.
- Screen time cap (<3 hrs/day) recurs across his regimens; reflects awareness of phone overuse as a discipline problem.
- No alcohol + no nicotine/vaping confirms the abstinence commitments seen in [[75 hard 2025]] and [[lock in]].
