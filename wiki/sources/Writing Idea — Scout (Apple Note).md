# Writing Idea — Scout (Apple Note)

> *"something about scout and how he was the one remainder from my past life with mom and dad"*

A one-sentence Apple Note — a companion seed to [[Writing Idea — Tilting Picture (Apple Note)]]. Both were jotted at roughly the same period and circle the same wound: what survives from the intact family that existed before the [[Family and Personal Life|divorce]].

## What this note is
- A private noticing: **Scout** (a pet — dog implied by name and context) as the single physical continuity between the pre-divorce family and the present.
- Unfinished; no corresponding realized essay has been ingested. May still be a future piece, or may have been folded into a broader work not yet in the wiki.

## Why it matters
The image is potent: if a pet is *"the one remainder,"* everything else — the household, the routine, the geography, the felt sense of family — has been lost or split. It names the divorce not as an event but as an erasure, with one surviving artifact.

Pairs with the [[Writing Idea — Tilting Picture (Apple Note)|tilting picture note]] as evidence of a writer circling a subject from multiple small angles before committing to prose.
