# Writing Idea — Scout (apple note)

A one-line writing-idea note from [[Traveler Stansberry]]'s Apple Notes:

> "writing idea: something about scout and how he was the one remainder from my past life with mom and dad"

## What it tells us

- **Scout** is a pet (almost certainly a dog, given the name) who belonged to the family before the [[Family and Personal Life|divorce]].
- Traveler frames Scout as a **living artifact** — the single physical thread connecting the pre-divorce household to the present. The word *remainder* is precise and telling: everything else from that life has been partitioned, reorganized, or lost.
- It's a **writing impulse, not a finished piece** — there is no companion essay in the corpus. That gap itself is notable: he identified the idea but (as of the available sources) never executed it. Possibly too raw.

## Connections
- Pairs with the other "past life" writing-idea note in the same batch — *why the photo of him with both parents always seems to lean toward his mom* — both circling the same pre-divorce household.
- Scout as object of memory belongs to the same emotional register as [[Memory and Trauma]], applied now not to literary characters but to a living animal.
- The "past life" framing recurs in his [[College Essay (Surfing)|college essay]] and the [[It's Not in My Control|avoidance essay]] — language of total discontinuity, not gradual change.
