# Subscription Pricing Tiers

A quick-reference note capturing three subscription tiers — Normal, Premium, and Pro — with their monthly and annual rates. Notably, the Premium annual plan offers no discount over monthly billing, while Normal offers the best proportional savings at 12.5% annually. Pro sits in the middle with a modest 7% annual discount.

## Key points
- **Normal**: $10/month or $80/year — 12.5% discount for annual
- **Premium**: $20/month or $180/year — 0% discount (no incentive to pay annually)
- **Pro**: $25/month or $210/year — 7% discount for annual
- Annual pricing anomaly: Premium charges the same effective rate regardless of billing cadence, making monthly the rational choice for that tier
- Pro is the highest tier but not the highest per-month price relative to value signaling
