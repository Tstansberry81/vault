# Writing Idea — Tilting Picture (Apple Note)

> *"why does the picture of me and my dad and mom lean everytime i come to my dads. it leans towards my mom"*

A one-sentence Apple Note. A seed, not a finished piece — but one that **germinated**. The observation became the structural spine of [[God (personal essay)|the *God* personal essay]], which opens: *"The picture always tilts towards my mom."*

## What this note is
- A private, unprompted observation recorded at [[Porter Stansberry (father)|his dad's]] house.
- No edits, no craft — just the raw noticing that something small and physical kept happening and felt weighted with meaning.
- Filed alongside companion seed: [[Writing Idea — Scout (Apple Note)]] ("Scout… the one remainder from my past life with mom and dad").

## Why it matters (even as a seed)
The note captures the instinct that precedes the essay: that an inanimate object — a family photo that won't stay straight — keeps quietly reorienting toward the absent parent. The finished *God* essay turns this into an extended meditation on his [[Faith vs. Knowledge|disbelief in God]], his [[Family and Personal Life|parents' divorce]], and what he lost in the rupture of his intact family.

That the picture "leans toward mom" at *dad's house* is the kind of detail that only has weight if there's grief behind it. He knew it and wrote it down before he knew what to do with it.
