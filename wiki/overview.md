**Scope:** 77 documents spanning four courses ([[English Curriculum 2022-2026|the curriculum]]): essays, responses, quizzes, study guides, memoirs, and one original short story. Sources are mostly Traveler's own writing, so the wiki doubles as a portrait of an evolving thinker.

**Key themes (by course):**

- **English 9 (2022–23):** Fate & free will; individual vs. society; identity; masculinity (toxic, performed).
- **English 10 (2023–24):** Slavery's moral weight; self-reliance vs. conformity; individual resistance.
- **IB HL English 1 (2024–25):** Power, hierarchy, justice; art as survival; memory and trauma; the rebel almost always loses.
- **IB HL English 2 (2025–26):** Identity and its collapse; the past that won't stay past; the artist who cannot escape; consumerism and alienation.

**Synthesis:** A consistent intellectual voice emerges — skeptical of power, drawn to outsiders, suspicious of easy answers, alert to how language and tradition are used to control. His essays show **strong argumentative clarity** and **readiness to sit in complexity**; his taste in literature runs to dark, unsettling works. The 12-page [[Narrow Road to the Deep North Response]] (senior year) is the crown jewel — it puts three literary frameworks in conversation with vivid precision.
