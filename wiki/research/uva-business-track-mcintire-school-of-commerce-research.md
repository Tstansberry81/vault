# UVA Business Track — McIntire School of Commerce

Researched July 30, 2026. Relevant to Trav: enrolls Fall 2026, so his McIntire application cycle is spring 2027, matriculating Fall 2027 (second year).

## The one structural fact that drives everything
There is no business major at UVA you can enroll into directly. McIntire is an **upper-divisional school** — you do a year in the College first, then apply. Direct entry and preferred/guaranteed admission for incoming first-years do not exist.

- Apply in the **spring of first year**; if admitted, you start Commerce coursework in **August of second year**.
- The 2026 cycle ran: application opened Jan 28, closed Apr 30 at noon ET, decisions June 12, accept/decline by June 19. Trav's cycle should mirror that in 2027 — assume a late-January open and a late-April deadline until McIntire posts 2027 dates.
- As of Fall 2025 the degree became a **three-year program** (previously two). Structure: ~15 credits of Foundational Core in second year → ~15 credits of Integrated Core in third year, cohort-based → concentration/track coursework in fourth year.
- Applying as a third-year is technically allowed but means a five-year bachelor's; the alternatives McIntire points at are the McIntire Business Institute (MBI) and the M.S. in Commerce.

## Admissions reality
- **Acceptance rate ~5.5% for the 2025 incoming class.** That is the number to internalize — it is one of the hardest undergrad business programs in the country to get into, harder than UVA itself. Being a UVA student is the entry ticket, not an advantage.
- Prerequisites must be done **by the time you enter in the fall**, not by the time you apply — but you must show a credible plan for anything outstanding.
- Minimum **54 credits** completed before entering, counting AP/test, transfer, and UVA courses.
- Prerequisite set (verify against the live checklist each year — it changes):
  - COMM 2010 + COMM 2020 (intro accounting sequence)
  - ECON 2010 + ECON 2020 (micro + macro)
  - Math + statistics. **AP/transfer credit no longer satisfies STAT 1120** — that one has to be taken at UVA.
  - COMM 1800
  - College of Arts & Sciences First Writing, Humanities/Engagements, and world language requirements. Language prereqs can be transferred in the summer before enrollment with prior approval.
- Two GPAs matter: cumulative, and a **quantitative GPA** calculated only from COMM 2010/2020, ECON 2010/2020, math and stats. That's the one that gets scrutinized.
- No published minimum GPA — review is holistic: academic performance and course rigor, course load, co-curriculars and work/volunteer experience, communication, teamwork, leadership, maturity. Echols Scholars are not exempt from the prereqs.
- McIntire's own stated keys to success: full 15-credit loads, rigor across disciplines, and **no more than two quantitative courses in one semester**.
- You can hold one additional major and one minor alongside Commerce — not two majors.

## The curriculum
- **Integrated Core Experience (ICE)** is the signature. Third year, team-taught, case-method, cohort-based: students are split into "blocks" of ~40-45 who stay together all year, then into study groups of 4-6 for project work. Faculty coordinate content across all core courses so accounting, finance, marketing, and strategy land on the same case.
- Case studies and business articles instead of textbooks; daily discussion-driven classes. It builds a generalist who can take an unstructured problem end to end.
- Corporate partners embed live problems. In the redesigned program, third-years do a **semester-long project with a partner company and present to its executives** at the end of the fall.
- **Concentrations (choose up to two):** Accounting, Finance, Information Technology, Management, Marketing.
- **Interdisciplinary tracks (up to two):** Analytics & AI, Entrepreneurship, Global Commerce & Society, Quantitative Finance, Real Estate, Strategic Brand Consulting & Communications. IT concentrators are encouraged to have a programming course under them.
- Minors at the Comm School include Entrepreneurship, Leadership, and Real Estate; non-Commerce majors can declare the General Business Minor.
- Study abroad is built in — semester, January term, or spring break formats.

## Outcomes (Class of 2025)
- Average starting salary **$94,747**; 78.7% reported a signing bonus averaging **$9,746**.
- **95.1%** of job-seeking grads placed within three months (94.1% in 2024).
- **93%** completed a business-specific internship before graduating — the internship is effectively part of the pipeline, not a bonus.
- Top employers: Deloitte, Capital One, KPMG, JP Morgan, Bank of America, Accenture, Bain, FTI Consulting, McKinsey, PwC.
- Poets&Quants ranked it 2nd of 110 in Admission Standards, 1st in Academic Experience, 11th in Career Outcomes. Average SAT of the incoming class 1478; six-year grad rate 99.2%.

## What this means tactically (my read, not McIntire's)
1. **First year is the whole game.** A ~5.5% admit rate off a one-shot spring application means first-year grades in five or six specific courses decide it. There is no recovery year.
2. **Sequence the quant courses deliberately.** COMM 2010/2020, ECON 2010/2020, STAT 1120 and a math all have to land inside first year while keeping to McIntire's own "max two quant courses per semester" advice. That is a tight schedule — build it before orientation, not during it.
3. **STAT 1120 must be taken at UVA.** Any AP stats credit is useless for the prereq, and it feeds the quantitative GPA. Don't let an advisor wave it off.
4. **Co-curriculars need to start in the fall of first year**, not the spring. The application asks for leadership and involvement, and you can't manufacture that in March.
5. **Have a plan B that isn't a downgrade.** A College major (Econ, Statistics, Computer Science) plus the General Business Minor, then the M.S. in Commerce, is a real path — and worth knowing before decisions come out in June, not after.
6. **Go to walk-in advising early.** McIntire explicitly runs advising for prospective applicants and flags it for anyone with a missing prereq. Free signal on how your file reads.
