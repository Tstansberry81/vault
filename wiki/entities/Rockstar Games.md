# Rockstar Games

Rockstar Games (a subsidiary of **Take-Two Interactive**) — the developer of the *Grand Theft Auto* franchise — appears in [[Traveler Stansberry]]'s wiki primarily as the **subject firm** of his [[Economics IA|SL Economics Microeconomics IA]].

## Role in the IA
- **Research question:** *How will the changes from Grand Theft Auto 5 to Grand Theft Auto 6 affect the total revenue Rockstar Games generates in 2025?*
- Traveler uses the GTA 5 → GTA 6 product transition as a demand-side shift event and models its effect on Rockstar / Take-Two total revenue.
- Projected figures used in the IA: ~$3 billion first-year sales; $100 price point.

## Why Traveler chose it
His interest in the gaming industry — documented extensively in [[Gaming and PC Setup]] (RDR2 crash fixes, high-end PC build) — made Rockstar a natural case study. The topic let him apply micro theory to a market he personally follows.

> [!note] Scope
> Rockstar is a coursework reference, not a company Traveler has invested in or analyzed beyond the IA. No deeper financial or strategic analysis of Take-Two is on record.
