## The Open Quant Question

Traveler entered UVA committed to a **Finance major + Math minor**, with an eye toward the **Quantitative Finance & Analytics track** that McIntire offers. The live question is:

1. **Does he pivot to a Math major + CS minor / AI focus** (heavier math/coding, lower finance depth), or
2. **Does he stick with Finance + Math minor** (broader finance education, math support, less coding density), or
3. **Does he pursue a hybrid / double-major**?

The decision hinges on **demonstrated coding ability** and **appetite for proof-heavy math** — neither of which is yet established at the UVA level. See [[Personal Quant Model]] and [[The Edge (trading model)]] for evidence of idea generation; see [[Homework Hatch (startup)]] for project execution with Josh. The wiki's honest assessment (from [[CLAUDE.md]]) is:

> [!warning] Honest calibration — unproven at scale
> Traveler has demonstrated **idea-level design** (model specs, strategy), **directed some coding via Cursor/AI** (built components, ran backtest), and **strong enthusiasm for quant**. He has **NOT** demonstrated the ability to **write production code from scratch alone**, solve novel algorithmic problems independently, or **weather heavy proof-based math** under academic pressure. Knowing ideas ≠ knowing math ≠ coding skill; directing an AI is not the same as coding.
>
> The Fall 2026 coursework (CS 1110, ECON 2010, PHIL 1730, COMM 1800) will be **the first real test** of whether he can execute at university scale. Excellence here would build the evidence for a Math pivot; struggle would suggest sticking with Finance.
