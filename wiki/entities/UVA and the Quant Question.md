> [!warning] Honest calibration: math vs. quant
> **Demonstrated:** strong abstract math ability (HL grades, can discuss and *direct* implementation of statistical models, understands Black-Scholes conceptually). 
> 
> **Untested:** hands-on coding of quant models (the AI did the writing). Can he *implement* from scratch? Unknown. His model is AI-generated from prompts; his own code skills are minimal/untested. Being able to *direct* an AI or *understand* the math ≠ being able to *build* the system solo. 
>
> **The gap:** if the quant question is about self-reliance and independent technical execution, that's a real gap. If it's about learning a field quickly with AI as a tool, he's probably fine. **Calibration:** strong on concepts, untested on hands-on execution.
