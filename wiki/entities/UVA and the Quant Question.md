- **Appetite:** Intellectually drawn to quant models, market psychology, automation; has sketched [[Personal Quant Model|a multi-factor equity model]] and directed a Flask app for it (code untested/unshipped)
- **Uncertainty:** *Coding implementation is the real gap.* He can design a quant system, but hasn't shipped production code solo. The model exists as a **proof of concept, not a proof of ability.** (See [[Personal Quant Model]] for honest calibration.)
