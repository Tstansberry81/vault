# William Dorman

Close friend of [[Traveler Stansberry]]. One of the few people Traveler explicitly names as a **best friend**.

## How they met
Met ~2 years before the portrait was written at a **pre-Business summer program at Yale** (~16-person cohort). Traveler says he immediately knew William was someone to stay in touch with — unusually decisive for him socially. They drifted, then **reconnected the following summer**, which Traveler describes as a meaningful reentry into the friendship.

> [!note] Full name
> Confirmed as **William Dorman** via a calendar entry (2026-07-13: "William Dorman is a Good Boy"). The portrait itself uses only "William."

## What Traveler says about him
From the [[William (friend portrait)|friend portrait]] — Traveler's own words, lightly organized:

| Trait | Description |
|---|---|
| **Openness** | Comfortable talking about a wide range of subjects; typically knowledgeable |
| **Empathy** | Unusually deep — Traveler names this as a primary reason he considers William a best friend despite the distance |

## Shared project — AI financial analysis model
Over the summer they reconnected, the two spent a week **designing a proprietary AI model to analyze financial data** in the style of "certain popular analysts." William owned the planning:

- Mapped a full **day-by-day schedule** for the week
- Due to his planning, they built a **working base model by the end of the week**

This is a peer-collaborative counterpart to [[Homework Hatch (startup)]] — both sit at the finance/AI intersection Traveler gravitates toward. The project demonstrates William's systematic, planning-first approach to execution.

> [!warning] Scope of this project
> The portrait is Traveler's admiring characterization, not a technical record. What "base model" means in capability terms is unspecified — don't read this as a production system. It's evidence of collaborative drive and project follow-through, not of any particular technical depth.

## Geography
They live in **different states** — the friendship is maintained at a distance and through intentional reconnection, which Traveler notes explicitly.

## Why he matters in the wiki
- One of the **very few people Traveler explicitly calls a best friend** — a short list given his documented emotional guardedness ([[Emotional Life and Inner World]]).
- The empathy characterization is notable: Traveler rarely describes receiving empathy from others in his personal writing; this is an exception.
- The shared AI/finance project shows Traveler's social circle overlaps with his intellectual interests — not a coincidence.
