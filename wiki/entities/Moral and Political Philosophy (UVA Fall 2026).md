## Syllabus: First Half (Aug 25 — Sep 17)

- **Themes:** Introduction to the field; foundational questions; relativism vs. universalism

- **Readings:**
  - Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics*, Books I–III.4
- **Themes:** Eudaimonia (flourishing); virtue ethics; the mean between extremes

- **Themes:** Courage, temperance, generosity, pride; types of friendship and their moral value
- **Calendar:** Sep 8 & 10, 3:30–4:20 PM
