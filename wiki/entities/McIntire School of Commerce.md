## Structure (Third-Year Entry)

McIntire uses a **third-year-entry model** — the first two years are prerequisite coursework, after which students apply formally to enter the Commerce major (typically ~50% admit rate in top cohorts, but the Finance concentration is competitive).

### Third Year (if admitted to Commerce)

Formal admission to McIntire's Commerce program. **Concentration options:**
- **Finance** (Traveler's target)
- **Consulting**
- **Management**
- **Information Technology**

McIntire also offers **specialized tracks**, including:
- **Quantitative Finance & Analytics** — the track Traveler is considering but has not yet committed to
- **AI/Business Applications**

### Finance Concentration Pathway

**Core Finance courses** (third year onward):
- COMM 3100 (or equivalent) — Financial Management / Corporate Finance
- COMM 3200 — Investments / Portfolio Management
- Electives: Fixed Income, Derivatives, Risk Management, Financial Modeling

**Optional Quantitative Finance track:**
- Advanced Math (Linear Algebra, Real Analysis, Probability/Statistics)
- Coding (Python, C++ for quant libraries; Traveler has some exposure)
- Specialization courses: Computational Finance, Asset Pricing, Algorithmic Trading
