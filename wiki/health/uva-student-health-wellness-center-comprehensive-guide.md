# UVA Student Health & Wellness Center - Comprehensive Guide

## Overview
<cite index="24-2,24-3">Student Health and Wellness at UVA is dedicated to promoting the health and wellbeing of all students, supporting their academic and personal success. Student Health and Wellness provides high-quality, integrated, and affordable care across primary health, counseling and mental health, well-being, and disability access services.</cite>

## Departments & Services

### Comprehensive Departments
<cite index="24-1">The UVA Department of Student Health and Wellness (SHW) includes — Care and Support Services (CASS), Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS), Medical Services, Well-being Services, the Student Disability Access Center (SDAC).</cite>

### Building Services & Resources
<cite index="1-2">The building is home to a UVA Health satellite Pharmacy and Laboratory, Counseling and Psychological Services, Medical Services, Well-Being Services, the Student Disability Access Center, Student Health Office of Research Excellence, the Gordie Center, the Fried Center for the Advancement of Potential, and Care and Support Services, and the SHW Radiology office.</cite>

### Medical Services
<cite index="7-1,7-2">Medical Services sees an average of 39,000 visits each academic year. The team of nurses, physicians, nurse practitioners, care managers, registered dietitians, radiology techs, and lab staff work collaboratively with other areas in Student Health and Wellness to deliver holistic medical care to students.</cite>

### Mental Health Services (CAPS - Counseling and Psychological Services)
<cite index="28-1,28-2">Provides one-on-one counseling, group therapy, and psychiatric services to UVA students at no cost. Offers free, brief individual therapy, group therapy, psychiatric medication services, 24-hour crisis consultation, outreach and prevention services, and care management.</cite>

<cite index="11-1">In addition to 1:1 appointments, CAPS provides 24/7 on-call crisis help, support group counseling, and have school-specific embedded counselors within most UVA schools.</cite>

**Mental Health Crisis Line:** <cite index="6-1">434-243-5150 (24/7)</cite>

### Telehealth Services

#### TimelyCare - 24/7 Virtual Mental Health
<cite index="16-2,16-3">CAPS partners with TimelyCare to offer UVA students 24/7 virtual, wraparound mental health care at no cost. Students do not need insurance to access TimelyCare.</cite>

<cite index="13-3">Any student who has paid UVA's comprehensive health fee will have ready access to the service, called TimelyCare, and can download the associated app to their phone or other device.</cite>

**TimelyCare Services Include:**
- <cite index="16-4,16-5">24/7, on-demand access to mental health care. Peer Community, an anonymous peer-to-peer support where students can use as a supportive space to connect with other students across the country who face similar challenges.</cite>
- <cite index="16-10,16-11,16-12">Private telehealth offices are available for students Mon-Fri 8:00am – 4:30pm. Call (434) 243-5150 to book.</cite>

**TimelyCare Support:** <cite index="16-9">Visit timelycare.com/uva to chat for technical support or call 1-833-4-TIMELY.</cite>

### Pharmacy
<cite index="29-4,29-5">The UVA Student Health Pharmacy offers retail prescriptions for patients. The pharmacist provides drug therapy review, patient education, drug information, and counseling for prescription and over-the-counter-medications in a private counseling area.</cite>

<cite index="29-6">The pharmacy has a selection of over-the-counter medications and healthcare products in the Atrium Apothecary.</cite>

## Appointments & Online Portal

### HealthyHoos Patient Portal
<cite index="15-1,15-2">HealthyHoos is a secure online patient portal that allows UVA students to schedule appointments online and have convenient, 24-hour access to personal health information. To login to HealthyHoos, students should use their Netbadge ID.</cite>

<cite index="15-6">Appointments available for Medical Services, Nutrition Services, Well-being Coaching with WahooWell, The Fried Center, and the Pharmacy, as well as first appointments with CAPS for mental health care.</cite>

<cite index="14-8">If you're trying to message your health care provider or upload a medical file, please use the HealthyHoos patient portal's secure messaging system.</cite>

## Cost & Insurance Information

<cite index="24-9,24-10">Teams serve all UVA undergraduate and graduate students charged the mandatory fees (which includes Student Health and Wellness) with their tuition. There are no costs associated with visits to care providers as they have been included with tuition as mandatory fees.</cite>

<cite index="24-11">However, there can be charges for medications, lab tests, supplies for certain treatments, immunizations and allergy injections.</cite>

## Transportation

### Parking
<cite index="1-4,1-6,1-7">The SHW parking garage is underground on Monroe Lane, which runs parallel to Brandon Ave. P1 parking is for students with appointments only. P2 parking is for staff/faculty with B3 permits only.</cite>

### Public Transit
<cite index="1-12,1-13,1-14">The University Transit Service (UTS) is a fare-free transit service committed to providing safe, reliable, efficient, and friendly transit service to University of Virginia students, faculty, and staff. UTS buses regularly drive by Brandon Ave, the closest SHW bus stop. Take the blue line, orange line, silver line or redline bus.</cite>

<cite index="1-15">Your stop is Jefferson Park Ave @ Brandon Ave. For real-time bus tracking, download the TransLoc Rider app or use TransLoc online to find the nearest bus stop, track your bus in real-time, see arrival time estimates, and receive service impact notifications.</cite>

## Equipment & Policy Notes

<cite index="1-9,1-10,1-11">In accordance with UVA Policy SEC-043, electric bikes, motorized scooters, and similar devices are not permitted inside Student Health and Wellness facilities. This includes storing, parking, or charging these devices anywhere within the building. Please use approved outdoor parking areas or bike racks.</cite>
