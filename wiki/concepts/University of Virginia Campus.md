# University of Virginia Campus

Historic grounds and infrastructure at the University of Virginia in Charlottesville, Virginia. [[Traveler Stansberry]] researched the campus ahead of arrival for orientation (July 2026).

## The Rotunda

**Thomas Jefferson's iconic centerpiece.** Designed by Jefferson, construction 1822–1826. Neo-classical architecture modeled on the Roman Pantheon; serves as the symbolic heart of the Academical Village.

**1895 fire:** Catastrophic fire damaged/destroyed major interior sections. Significant restoration necessary.

**Restoration history:**
- Post-fire restoration by Stanford White
- 1970s restoration returned the building closer to Jefferson's original design
