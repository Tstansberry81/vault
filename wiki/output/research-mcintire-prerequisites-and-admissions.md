# Research McIntire prerequisites and admissions

Saved to the vault: **wiki/research/mcintire-spring-semester-course-plan-first-year.md**

Here's the full research writeup:

## Big finding first
McIntire just overhauled its whole model (effective for students who started Fall 2024+, which includes Trav). It went from a two-year, direct-third-year-entry program to a **three-year program**, and the prerequisite list got dramatically shorter. Accounting and statistics are **no longer prerequisites** — they moved into the McIntire core curriculum itself (taken *after* admission). Only three prerequisites remain: **COMM 1800 (Foundations of Commerce), ECON 2010 (Microeconomics), and the First Writing Requirement (FWR)**.

## What that means for Trav
He's already knocking out ECON 2010 this fall. If he takes **COMM 1800 + an FWR course** in spring, he'll have **all three McIntire prerequisites done by the end of his first year** — well ahead of schedule.

**Recommended Spring 2027 schedule (~15-16 credits):**
- COMM 1800 – Foundations of Commerce (3 cr) — prereq
- FWR-satisfying writing seminar (3-4 cr) — prereq
- ECON 2020 – Macroeconomics (3 cr) — not required, but keeps his quant/business GPA strong and pairs naturally with micro
- A Gen-Ed/Area requirement course (3 cr) — McIntire likes seeing liberal-arts breadth
- Optional 5th course (e.g., continuing Calc) only if it doesn't overload him

**Admissions context:** Fully holistic review (GPA + rigor + course load + extracurriculars/leadership/work experience). The 2026 cycle admitted at a 43.5% rate with a 3.9 mean GPA — competitive and trending up.

**Timeline:** Prereqs done Spring 2027 → apply that same spring (~Jan–April, exact dates TBD) → decision ~June 2027 → McIntire core in his 2nd year → Integrated Core Experience in his 3rd year → normal 4-year graduation.

**Pitfalls flagged:** don't plan around outdated prereq lists still floating around online/in some UVA pages, don't over-cram now-unnecessary courses, watch out for CR/NC grading on prereqs, and lean on official McIntire advising since this is a transition year with little peer precedent.

**Flagged as unverified** (couldn't confirm from official sources): exact 2027 application dates, any hard minimum-grade rule on prereqs, AP/IB credit substitution specifics, and a discrepancy between two official McIntire pages — one still lists the old, longer prerequisite set. I'd recommend Trav confirm that discrepancy directly with McIntire advising.
