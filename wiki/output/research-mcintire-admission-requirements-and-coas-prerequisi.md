### **1. MCINTIRE ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS FOR INTERNAL TRANSFER STUDENTS (CoAS → McIntire)**

#### **Application Timeline & Process**

Beginning with academic year 2024-2025, first-year students will be expected to apply to McIntire's new three-year curriculum. Students who are in their first year in 2024-2025 and future first-year students will be eligible to apply for admission to the B.S. in Commerce Program in April or May of their first year, for matriculation in the fall of their second year.
For 2026 applicants: B.S. in Commerce application opened on Wednesday, Jan. 28, 2026. Application closed on Thursday, April 30, 2026, at noon ET. Decisions were released on Friday, June 12, 2026. Students have until noon ET on Friday, June 19, 2026, to accept or decline the offer.


#### **Admission Statistics & Acceptance Rate**

The 2026 admission cycle saw an increase in the mean admitted student GPA from 3.86 in last year's first-year admission cycle to 3.9 this year. Of the 381 students who received offers, 372 students accepted their offer, making the matriculation rate 97.6 percent.
The admission rate for the 2026 McIntire admission cycle saw a decline when compared with last year's admission rate for first-years applying into the three-year McIntire program — which had an admission rate of 44 percent.
 Recent data shows a 43.5% acceptance rate for 2026 cycle.

#### **GPA Thresholds & Criteria**

GPA is only one factor in the Admission Committee's review of applicants. There is no minimum GPA, as the committee evaluates multiple criteria in the holistic evaluation process.
 However, 
the students who ultimately transfer in as third-years have strong transcripts filled with rigorous coursework. You should be aiming for top grades across the board, particularly in quantitative classes like calculus and economics, which McIntire requires for admission.


#### **Holistic Review Criteria**

Factors taken into consideration include academic performance (GPA, performance in courses related to business, degree of difficulty of courses, and course load taken); co-curricular activities including volunteer and work experience; and evidence of solid communication, teamwork, leadership, and personal skills.

### **2. REQUIRED PREREQUISITE COURSES FOR MCINTIRE ADMISSION**

#### **Core Prerequisites (Required Before Enrollment)**

The number of prerequisites has been significantly reduced. Students are now only required to complete three courses before applying — COMM 1800, "Foundations of Commerce," ECON 2010, "Principles of Microeconomics" and the College's First Writing Requirement.


Breaking this down:
- **COMM 1800** (Foundations of Commerce / Introduction to Business) — 3 credits
- **ECON 2010** (Principles of Microeconomics) — 3 credits
- **First Writing Requirement** (English Composition/ENWR 1510 or equivalent) — 3 credits

#### **Additional Preferred/Recommended Courses**

Before entering the McIntire School of Commerce, you must complete a minimum of 54 credits and complete all prerequisites by the fall of your third year. This includes any test credits, transfer credits, and courses taken at UVA.
We recommend students make progress toward the General Education Requirements in their first year to be a competitive applicant.


#### **Quantitative Coursework Emphasis**

In short, no you do not need to take a higher-level math or Econ to apply to McIntire. There is a misconception that higher-level Calculus or Economics classes are the "unannounced requirements." Fortunately, that is exactly what it is: a misconception!


However, 
Avoid taking more than two quantitative courses in the same semester.


#### **No Additional Pre-Enrollment Upper-Level Courses Required**

COMM 1800, 2010, and 2020 are the only courses with the COMM mnemonic that are required prior to enrollment. COMM 3410 Commercial Law may be taken before enrollment and is included in students' 39 required Commerce hours if taken at UVA. No other 3000- or 4000-level courses taken before enrollment may be used to satisfy Commerce major requirements.

### **3. APPLICATION STRATEGY & COMPETITIVENESS RECOMMENDATIONS**

#### **Key Advisor Recommendations for First-Year Applicants**

Take full course loads (15 credits). Challenge yourself with rigorous courses across many disciplines/areas, building your oral and written communication skills, ethics and moral reasoning, global perspective, quantitative skill set, and creative and critical-thinking abilities.


#### **What McIntire Looks For Beyond Academics**

In addition to their academic credentials, admitted students demonstrated outstanding co-curricular experiences that included a variety of involvement and leadership positions on and off Grounds.
When reviewing application materials, we look at traditional empirical measures of academic achievement, but we also look for things that indicate emotional intelligence, community engagement, and intellectual curiosity.

### **4. SPECIAL NOTES FOR INTERNAL CoAS STUDENTS**

**Direct-Admit Process for External Transfers (Not Applicable to CoAS):**

Transferring to McIntire is a direct-admit process, which means that admission to UVA is contingent upon being offered admission to McIntire.
 This applies only to external transfer students from other institutions, not to current CoAS students.

**No Hard GPA Cutoff:**

GPA is only one factor in the Admission Committee's review of applicants. There is no minimum GPA, as the committee evaluates multiple criteria in the holistic evaluation process.
